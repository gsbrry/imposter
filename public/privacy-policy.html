Impostr privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026. Applies to Impostr version 1.0 for Android.

Impostr is a party word game played on one phone. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features. The game itself works entirely offline, and the names and words you use while playing never leave your phone.

What we collect

Three things, and only these:

DataWhenLinked to you?
Crash logs and diagnostics Only if the app crashes or hits an error No
Purchase history Only if you buy or restore a premium plan Yes, to your Google account
Support reports Only when you choose to send one Yes, by your email address

What we do not collect

  • Player names, secret words and clues. These stay in your phone's storage and are never sent anywhere.
  • Your stats, settings and saved player names. Also stored only on your phone.
  • Advertising IDs. Impostr shows no ads and has no advertising identifier.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera. The app never asks for these permissions.
  • Analytics about how you use the app. There is no analytics tool in this version.

Why we collect it

  • Crash logs so we can find and fix bugs that break a game.
  • Purchase history so your premium plan works, can be restored on a new phone, and can be refunded.
  • Support reports so we can answer you and reproduce the problem you hit.

We do not sell any data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who processes it

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) receives crash reports. We have personal data collection turned off, so reports carry no IP address, no device identifier and no username. Player names and the game's words are stripped from every report before it is sent. Sentry privacy policy
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) manages subscriptions and receives your purchase and a pseudonymous app user ID. RevenueCat privacy policy
  • Google Play takes the payment and holds the billing record. We never see your card details. Google privacy policy
  • Email: support reports you send arrive in a Gmail inbox we control. Nothing in between stores them.

Everything above travels over HTTPS. These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Crash logs: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records: for the life of the subscription, then as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually seven years.
  • Support emails: up to 24 months after the conversation ends.
  • Anything on your phone: until you delete it in the app or uninstall Impostr. Uninstalling removes all of it.

Your choices

  • Play without ever sending anything: the game needs no connection.
  • Send a support report without device details by turning off the toggle on the report screen.
  • Ask us to delete your support emails and crash reports by writing to the address below. We will confirm within 30 days.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.

Children

Impostr is rated for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us a support report, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we add a feature that collects something new, we will update this page and the Data safety section of our Play Store listing before that feature ships. The date at the top shows when this page last changed.

Contact

Email gsbrry@gmail.com with any privacy question, or to ask for your data to be deleted. We reply within 2 working days.
