Privacy Policy for Impostr

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Impostr is a party word game published by Gooseberry Media. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app. The short version: the game runs entirely on your device, and we do not collect, see, or store anything you do in it.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. Impostr has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of our own, and no analytics. We never see your data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so the game works between sessions. This information stays on your phone, is not transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Player names you enter
  • Game statistics, such as rounds played and rounds won
  • Your settings, such as timer, sound and haptics preferences
  • Which words have already come up, so they do not repeat
  • Category suggestions you write in the “Suggest a Category” screen

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or a one-off upgrade, the purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address, or any other payment details.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and to confirm whether a purchase is active. When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives:

  • Your purchase history for this app
  • An anonymous app user identifier generated for your installation
  • Your country and currency, so prices display correctly

RevenueCat processes this on our behalf to deliver what you paid for and to give us aggregate sales figures. It is encrypted in transit. We do not use it to advertise to you, and we do not sell or share it with anyone else.

RevenueCat’s privacy policy · Google’s privacy policy

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No location data of any kind
  • No access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or files
  • No data sold or shared with data brokers

Children

Impostr is a general-audience party game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because we collect nothing at all, no personal information from a child can reach us.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. To erase it, clear the app’s storage in your device settings, or uninstall the app.

To request deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat, email us at the address below and we will action it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the app changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
