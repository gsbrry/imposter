Impostr · Redesign concept · 16 screens

Honey, cream & liquid glass

A calm, premium take on the party word game. Frosted glass panels float over warm honey light, with one bold accent and tighter corners. Switch palettes to compare the three colour directions.

Colour direction

Why honey, not green

Warm yellow reads as celebration and value, and pairs with cream and ink for a quiet, expensive feel. Green made the old app look like a utility.

Glass with a job

Glass is reserved for floating layers: cards, sheets and the tab bar. Primary actions stay solid so the next step is always obvious.

Less curve, more calm

Corners drop from 28px to 14–20px. One accent per screen, ink for selected states, and rose only when the imposter is revealed.

Fewer taps per round

Setup is three clear steps with a progress bar. In-game screens hide everything except the one action the holder of the phone needs.
