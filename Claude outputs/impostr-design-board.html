App icon

A speech bubble for the clues, with three dots. One of them is a watching eye: the imposter hiding among friends. Reads clearly from 1024px down to 48px.

Core game

Build now

16 screens that replace the current app. Same game, cleaner flow.

Responsive check

Headings, the timer ring and the grids scale with screen width. On short phones the content scrolls and the main button stays at the bottom of the content. Open any file in the screens folder and resize the window to test.

Online play

Future

Everyone plays on their own phone. The host shares a link in the group chat, friends tap it, install or open the app, and land straight in the room. No passing the phone.

Ad placements

Planned

One small card-sized ad, only where it cannot interrupt the game. It appears after the player scrolls to the bottom, or where the screen already has empty space.

Where ads may appear

  • Home, below the categories
  • Result, below Play again
  • Stats and Settings, at the bottom

Where ads never appear

  • Pass the phone, word reveal, clue round, vote
  • Premium, setup steps, online rooms
  • Never as a pop-up or full-screen video

The ad card

  • Native ad, full content width, about 76px tall
  • Clearly marked AD, same corners as other cards
  • Hidden completely for Pro users

Web version

On a laptop the app sits in a centred column with the same look. The side panels help people who arrive from an invite link: get the app, or join with a code. On phones and narrow windows only the app column shows.
